A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Good to know.

How do I know the final price?

The prices listed are starting prices. We confirm the exact quote with you before any work begins.

How long will delivery take?

Alterations usually take 4–24 hours, blouse and kurti stitching 2–3 days, and suits and coats 3–5 days. We’ll confirm the timing when you book.

How do you take measurements?

Share your reference garment or measurement notes at pickup. We’ll call you if the tailor needs any details.

How do you keep my fabric safe?

Each pickup is tagged and handed over directly to the tailor. Your fabric stays carefully tracked through delivery.

What if I need a refund or a fix?

If the fit needs a small correction, request a free fix within 48 hours. For cancellations or refunds, contact our team and we’ll help based on the order status.
